Mariana Enriquez

The queen of Argentine horror

Who she is

Mariana Enriquez was born in Buenos Aires in 1973. She studied journalism at the National University of La Plata and is the deputy editor of Radar, the culture supplement of the newspaper Página/12.

Her stories mix classic horror (ghosts, curses, haunted houses) with the real terror of Argentina: the disappeared of the last dictatorship, poverty and violence

Books to start with

  1. Las cosas que perdimos en el fuego (2016)
  2. Los peligros de fumar en la cama (2009)
  3. Nuestra parte de noche (2019)

Why I love her work

  • She makes Buenos Aires feel both familiar and terrifying.
  • Her horror always has something to say about history.
  • Her short stories are perfect to read in one sitting.
