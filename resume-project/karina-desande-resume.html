About Me

Adventure defines me: I love letting life surprise me, and I bring my full energy, enthusiasm, and commitment to every project. Education has been the thread running through my life, both as a student and as an educator. I have taught from elementary school to university, and I have met each new challenge with formal training and new degrees, most recently as a Ph.D. candidate in Latin American Literature at the University of Maryland. Living in Argentina, Brazil, and the United States and traveling as part of my growth have shaped the way I teach Spanish language, literature, and cultures. My wonderful family has been by my side every step of the way, encouraging me to keep exploring.

Experience

Instructor of Record

University of Maryland | 2022-Present

Since fall 2022, I have served as Instructor of Record in the Department of Spanish and Portuguese at the University of Maryland, teaching 100-, 200-, and 300-level courses. This semester, I am teaching Latin American Literatures and Cultures III: From Modernism to Neo-Liberalism, and I have developed case studies and a gamified approach in Spanish for Business that connects language learning with real professional situations.

Graduate Assistant

University of Maryland | 2024-2025

The Chair of the Department of Spanish and Portuguese selected me as his assistant for the 2024–2025 academic year. I coordinated the logistics of the Cátedra Pacheco, the department’s main annual event, and of campus visits by writers such as Yuri Herrera, Selva Almada, and Federico Falco. I also organized student activities for Maryland Day and for the Spanish Club, a year-round program for students. In addition, I redesigned the main office and the Sala Pacheco conference room and purchased furniture, merchandise, and supplies for the department.

Spanish Teacher

Glenelg High School | 2014-2022

As a Spanish teacher, I focused on creating an engaging classroom environment, drawing on approaches such as the flipped classroom, backward curriculum design, and multiliteracies. My students learned through hands-on activities: developing fictional characters, creative writing, singing, and games. I also co-chaired the Spanish National Honor Society and sponsored the Girl Up Club.

Spanish Teacher

Escuela Argentina | 2009-2019

I taught Spanish in 7th and 10th through 12th grades at Escuela Argentina, where I fostered a lively space for learning language, literature, and cultures. I redesigned the 10th- and 11th-grade curriculum to prepare students for AP Spanish Literature: every student passed, and many earned the top score of 5. The program is still in use today.

Spanish Instructor

Johns Hopkins University | 2009-2010

At the Johns Hopkins University Odyssey Program, a continuing education program for adults, I taught Spanish at the Intermediate I & II levels to health professionals such as doctors and nurses. My personalized approach helped them build the language skills to connect with Spanish-speaking patients and make each interaction more meaningful.
